Refund & Cancellation Policy

DeepEx · Last updated: 4 November 2026

Cancellation

  • You can cancel your subscription at any time from Profile → Cancel subscription in the app.
  • After cancellation, no further monthly amount will be debited. You can keep using the app until the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • If you cannot cancel in the app, email or WhatsApp us and we will cancel it for you within 2 working days.

Refunds

  • Subscription fees (including discounted coupon payments) for a period that has already started are non-refundable, because the digital content is available immediately.
  • Exceptions: if you were charged twice by mistake, charged after you had cancelled, or the payment was deducted but the subscription did not start, we will refund the extra or failed amount.
  • To request a refund, contact us within 7 days with your registered email/mobile number and payment details.

Refund timeline

Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank.

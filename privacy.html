Privacy Policy

DeepEx · Last updated: 4 November 2026

DeepEx ("we", "our") is a learning app for children, operated by Pradeep Kumar, Bhiwandi, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it is protected. The app account is created and managed by a parent or guardian.

1. Information we collect

  • Parent account: name, mobile number, email address and password (stored securely by our authentication provider).
  • Child profile: child's first name or nickname, age group and learning progress (lessons completed, daily listening time), entered by the parent.
  • Consent record: the date and time the parent agreed to the use of the child's data.
  • Subscription and payment status: plan status, renewal date and payment references. We do not see or store your card, UPI PIN or bank details; these are handled by Razorpay.
  • Feedback: messages you choose to send us.

2. How we use it

  • To create accounts, show lessons and save progress.
  • To manage subscriptions, coupons and payments.
  • To contact you about your account, payments or support requests.
  • To improve the app.

We do not sell personal data and we do not show third-party advertising to children.

3. Parental consent and children

Children cannot create accounts. A parent or guardian must sign up, give consent and manage the child profile. A parent can ask us at any time to review or delete the child's data.

4. Service providers

We use Supabase (database and login), Razorpay (payments) and a video/audio hosting provider to deliver lessons. They process data only to provide their service to us.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep data while the account is active. You can delete your account and all child profiles from the Profile screen in the app, or email us and we will delete it. Payment records may be kept as long as required by Indian law.

6. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and access is restricted. No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
